# About
I am a postdoctoral researcher at **<a href="https://www.hku.hk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The University of Hong Kong (HKU)</a>**, working with **<a href="https://www.ai.hku.hk/people/academic-staff/mayi/biography" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Yi Ma</a>**.

My research focuses on **embodied intelligence** and the **edge deployment of large foundation models**. I study how robots can learn from interaction and adapt to real-world environments, and how foundation models can operate efficiently on devices with limited memory and computational resources. By bringing together robot learning, control, and efficient model inference, I aim to enable embodied systems that can learn, plan, and act with intelligence deployed directly on the device.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in **<a href="https://idsc.ethz.ch/research-zeilinger.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Melanie Zeilinger’s group</a> at <a href="https://ethz.ch/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ETH Zurich</a>**. I completed my PhD through the **<a href="https://learning-systems.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Center for Learning Systems</a>**, a joint doctoral program between the **<a href="https://is.mpg.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS)</a>** and **<a href="https://ethz.ch/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ETH Zurich</a>**, advised by **<a href="https://sites.google.com/view/mmuehlebach/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Dr. Michael Muehlebach</a>** and **<a href="https://idsc.ethz.ch/research-zeilinger.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Melanie Zeilinger</a>**.
