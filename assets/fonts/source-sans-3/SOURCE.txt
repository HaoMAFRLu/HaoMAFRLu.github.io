The WOFF2 files are unchanged Latin and Latin Extended subsets from the package.
Each subset has separate normal and italic files and a continuous weight axis
from 200 to 900, including weights 400, 600 and 700. These are weight-only
variable files; they have no optical-size (opsz) axis. Declare the family name
"Source Sans 3", the correct style, and font-weight: 200 900 in CSS.

Verified from the WOFF2 name, fvar, OS/2 and cmap tables: correct font family,
weight range, normal/italic flags, and coverage of English plus accented
European names including Rene (René), Zurbrügg, Büchler, Schölkopf, Zürich,
François, Łódź, Čížek, Østergaard and Dvořák. Unicode ranges below are the
package CSS ranges; a range does not imply every code point has a glyph.
